Questions, answered

Is root canal treatment painful?

Modern RCT is done under local anaesthesia, so most patients feel little more than a regular filling. It's usually the infection beforehand that hurts — treatment brings relief.

Can I get both dental and skin treatment in one visit?

Yes — that's the idea behind Smile & Glow. Tell us both concerns when you book and we'll plan your visit accordingly.

Do I really need expensive skincare?

Not necessarily. Many affordable products work well when chosen for your skin type. Dr. Pallavi will suggest a routine that fits your skin and your budget.

Is there downtime after a chemical peel?

Light peels usually have little to no downtime — perhaps mild redness or flaking for a few days. You'll get clear aftercare instructions, including sun protection.

Can I consult in Hindi?

Of course — consultations are available in both Hindi and English.

Missing tooth: implant, bridge or denture?

All three fill the gap. They differ in how long they last, whether neighbouring teeth are touched, and cost.

Swipe the table to see all options →

ImplantBridgeDenture
How it worksA titanium post in the jaw holds a crownA crown on each side holds a false toothRemovable plate with false teeth
Neighbouring teethNot touchedTrimmed to hold the bridgeNot trimmed; clasps may rest on them
Time3–6 months (healing)2–3 visits2–4 visits
LastsMany years, often decades with careAround 10–15 yearsNeeds relining or replacing every few years
CostFrom ₹20,000 per toothAsk at your check-upAsk at your check-up
  • Choose an implant if you want the closest thing to a natural tooth, your gums are healthy and you don't smoke heavily.
  • Choose a bridge if the teeth on both sides already need crowns, or you want a fixed tooth faster.
  • Choose a denture if several teeth are missing or you need the most affordable option.

Toothache: filling, root canal or extraction?

The right treatment depends on how deep the decay goes — an X-ray shows this.

WhenDecay hasn't reached the nerveInfection or decay has reached the nerve (pulp)Tooth can't be saved
Typical signsSensitivity to sweet or coldLingering pain, pain at night, swelling, pain on bitingBadly broken tooth, very loose tooth

  • Don't wait if pain lingers or wakes you at night — treating early can mean a simple filling instead of a root canal.
  • Saving the tooth with RCT is almost always better than pulling it and replacing it later.

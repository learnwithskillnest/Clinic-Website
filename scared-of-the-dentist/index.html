Scared of the dentist?

You're not alone — and you're in charge. Here's exactly what to expect.

Feeling nervous about the dentist is very common — many adults put off treatment for years because of it. The good news: modern dentistry is far gentler than most people remember, and you stay in control the whole time.

What happens at your first visit

  1. A chat first. Tell us what worries you — pain, needles, the sound of the drill, or a bad past experience. Nothing is done until you're ready.
  2. A gentle check-up. We look at your teeth and gums, and take an X-ray only if it's needed.
  3. Your options, explained. What needs doing now, what can wait, how many visits and what it costs. You decide.
  4. Treatment can be on another day. If you'd rather just meet us first, that's completely fine.

How pain is controlled

  • A numbing gel is applied to the gum before any injection, so you barely feel it.
  • Local anaesthesia numbs the area fully — most patients feel pressure or vibration, not pain.
  • We check that you're numb before starting, and add more if you need it.
  • After treatment, you'll know exactly what to expect and which pain relief to take.

You stay in control

  • Agree a stop signal — raise your hand and we pause straight away.
  • Ask for breaks whenever you need to rest your jaw.
  • Ask us to explain each step before we do it — or to stay quiet, if you'd rather not know.
  • Bring headphones and listen to music during treatment.
  • Bring someone you trust to sit with you.

Tips that help

  • Book a time when you're not rushed — evenings at Aashiyana suit many working patients.
  • Eat a light meal before, unless told otherwise; avoid too much tea or coffee.
  • Slow breathing helps: in for 4 counts, out for 6.
  • Start small — a cleaning or check-up first builds confidence before bigger treatment.
  • Don't wait for pain. Small problems need small treatment; waiting usually makes treatment longer.

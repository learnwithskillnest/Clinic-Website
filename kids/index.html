Kids' dental corner

Healthy milk teeth, happy visits — with a free brushing chart and a brave-patient certificate to print.

When to start

  • First dental visit: by the first birthday, or when the first tooth appears.
  • Then a check-up every 6 months.
  • Milk teeth matter — they hold space for adult teeth and help children eat and speak.

Brushing by age

  • Under 3: a smear of fluoride toothpaste, the size of a grain of rice.
  • 3–6 years: a pea-sized amount. Teach them to spit, not rinse.
  • Brush twice a day for 2 minutes. Parents should help until about age 7–8.

Preparing for the first visit

  • Keep it positive — avoid words like "hurt", "injection" or "pain".
  • Play "dentist" at home: count their teeth with a toothbrush.
  • Book a morning or after-nap time when your child is fresh.
  • Stay calm yourself — children pick up on worry.

Habits that protect teeth

  • No milk or juice bottle at bedtime.
  • Water between meals; sweets only after meals.
  • Ask us about fluoride varnish and sealants for back teeth.
  • Thumb-sucking beyond age 4–5? Mention it at the check-up.
